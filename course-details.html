من الأزياء إلى الأعمال: نحو براند عالمي

Fashion to Business: From Fashion Concept to Global Brand

بداية الدورة
أبريل 2026
عدد المقاعد
10 كحد أقصى
الدورات
2 Courses
إجمالي الساعات
60 Hours
Advanced Brand Development

Transition from designer to strategic brand manager

Objective: To transition students from designers to strategic brand managers capable of creating export-ready luxury products

Course Content:

  • The HOV Genesis: How to define a unique market position. Analysis of the Dubai fashion landscape and identifying gaps in the luxury market
  • Wearer-Centric Development: Moving beyond the sketch to consider movement and the "camera eye". Advanced draping and silhouette techniques used in HOV collections
  • Digital-First Resilience: How to disrupt a saturated market with limited resources. Social media storytelling and the "Hero Piece" strategy to generate buzz
  • Atelier Management: Sourcing international-standard materials and implementing quality control (QC) for export. Finishing techniques that define luxury
  • The Collection Pitch: Navigating the international fashion calendar and runway logistics
  • Submission: Students will present their physical "Hero Piece" alongside a full brand identity and digital launch strategy
30 Hours
EGP 8,000
Strategic Event Conceptualization

Master the event as a communication platform

Objective: To master the event as a communication platform that generates media value and B2B networking opportunities

Course Content:

  • Content-First Design: Engineering "shareable" moments and designing for the camera. Crafting professional media kits that attract international press
  • Atmospheric Scenography: Transforming venues through lighting, sound, and scent to match the brand story
  • Living Ambassadors: Casting for "attitude" and "identity" rather than just aesthetics. Training staff to embody the brand
  • The Strategic Guest List: The science of tiered guest lists and attracting buyers/investors. Creating "zones" to facilitate high-value B2B networking
  • The Execution: High-stakes logistics, run-of-show planning, and contingency management
30 Hours
EGP 7,000
